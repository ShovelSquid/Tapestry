In the cup example, "it lay shattered" fills in intact = false at @40. Was the cup broken at @0 too?
My take: a filled-in value counts from the moment the key is placed. Before that, the value stays unspecified. Going from unspecified to known is not a change, so it needs no cause. This keeps principle 5 as it is and stops descriptions from reaching back in time.
