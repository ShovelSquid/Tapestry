<objective>
Make the real-time face tracker in the hands-face-voice playground feel expressive instead of just geometrically tracked: turn on MediaPipe's semantic blendshape output and surface it on the page, and switch the 3D face point cloud from the coarse contour subset to the full tessellation mesh the 2D overlay already draws.

Purpose: the tracker currently only exposes raw mesh geometry (and, in 3D, only a ~132-point contour subset of that geometry) — there is no semantic expression signal anywhere in the pipeline, and the 3D view discards most of the expression-carrying detail (cheeks, nose, inner mouth) the model already returns. Both gaps are why the face tracker "doesn't feel expressive" despite full landmark tracking already working.

Output: `hands-face.js` requests `outputFaceBlendshapes: true`, plumbs the result through `onLandmarks`, and prints the top-scoring blendshapes into the existing metrics readout. `scene3d.js` builds its face point cloud from the full `FACE_LANDMARKS_TESSELATION` index set.
