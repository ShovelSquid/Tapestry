### Architecture

- FloatingToolbar positions itself above the selection using `view.coordsAtPos`
- Submenus open on hover, close after 300ms delay via `onMouseLeave`/`onMouseEnter` with timer (D-25)
- All commands use `onMouseDown` with `preventDefault()` to preserve text selection (D-24)
- toolbar-commands.ts wraps ProseMirror commands and provides active-state queries
- Component is view-agnostic — works with any EditorView from NoteCard or ThreadCenterNode (D-26)
