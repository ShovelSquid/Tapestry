### Architecture Decisions

- **Passage marks use ProseMirror marks with `excludes: ""`** to permit overlapping instances, each carrying a unique `anchorId` attribute
- **Thread center nodes** are kernel nodes with auto-positioning at endpoint midpoint; dragging pins them (`pinned: true`)
- **Shared useProseMirror hook** consumed identically by NoteCard and ThreadCenterNode, proving D-26 universal editing
- **Thread topology** uses thread-arm edges connecting through the center node
