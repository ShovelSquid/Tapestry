<objective>
The vault tree follows the vault live. Edits made in Obsidian, or by other processes, are recorded within about a second (D-20). They are grouped per moment (D-23) and carry honest provenance: `obsidian.bridge` when the author is unknown (D-21), `agent.<name>` when a sign-in log line proves an agent wrote that exact content (D-09). A rename keeps the note (D-24); a delete cuts its connections while history keeps everything (D-25).

Purpose: the source-mirror model (D-10) holds only if the tree records what the files say, promptly and with honest authorship. The watcher is only a hint; the hash-based reconciler decides what changed (RESEARCH Pattern 3).
Output: VaultWatcher, QuietWindowQueue, rename/delete reconciliation, attribution with the sign-in log (parse side), addon getCommits, recorded-change statuses, restart watching, and the sign-in log section of the reader's guide.

Recorded choices:
- D-23 "the same moment" means 750 ms quiet, with a forced flush after 5 s.
- Renames while the app runs are matched by content hash or inode; during catch-up only by hash. A rename plus an edit while closed becomes delete + create.
- The own-write echo hook defaults to "none" here; Plan 11 wires the writer into it.

File count: 22 files. The watcher, the quiet-window queue, the reconciler extension and the attribution path form one pipeline that cannot be observed working until every stage exists; its tests and the status surfaces are the remainder.
