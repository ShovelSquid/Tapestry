<objective>
Close review warning WR-02 (plan truth T-2.6-24). Today `openIntoSpace` in index.ts rolls the registry back only when `space.addMember` throws. `vaultService.addVault` can fail after it has already changed the registry, in two ways:

- an unavailable vault tree is recorded by `tryOpen`, and then `requireOpen` throws;
- the tree is adopted, and then `catchUp` rejects.

In both cases the entry stays in the registry with no stand-in. It is listed at (0, 0), any drop that touches it fails with `Unknown tree`, and it disappears at the next launch.

This plan moves the rollback into one small, tested helper, `openWithRollback`. It snapshots the registry's ids, runs `open()` and then `record()`, and on any failure closes every entry that is new since the snapshot and joins no stand-in. Checking the stand-in (`SpaceService.isMember`) keeps the rollback from closing an entry the forest already holds. Such an entry can come from a concurrent add that succeeded during a slow vault catch-up, or from a stand-in written before a later step failed. Closing either would break the same invariant in the other direction.

1. **Tracer (Task 1):** the helper, `SpaceService.isMember`, and the index.ts wiring for `trees:open`, `trees:create` and `vault:add`, proven with the reproduced vault failure: an unavailable record is made inside `open()`, then `open()` throws.
2. **Task 2:** the remaining rollback cases and `isMember`'s own tests.

It depends on Plan 08, which changes the join (`memberFor`) that `isMember` reads and shares `space-service.ts` and `membership.test.ts`.

Requirements: the phase owns none, so `requirements` is empty.
</objective>
