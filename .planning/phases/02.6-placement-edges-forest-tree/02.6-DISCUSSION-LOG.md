## Claude's Discretion

- The forest is not drawn inside itself, and the Tapestry tree is not drawn in 2.6.
- Opening or closing a tree is one commit under the user. The digest is recorded as `system` on first open.
- A moved file is re-identified only when reopened from its new path; there is no filesystem search.
- An unavailable member's frame can be moved.
- A frame undo is a compensating commit rather than a rewind, because `KernelBridge.submit` refuses commits while a tree is rewound.
