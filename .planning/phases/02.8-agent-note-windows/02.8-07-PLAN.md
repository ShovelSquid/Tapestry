<objective>
Forks, main half: a fork at an earlier completed turn k is one commit (the new session note plus its `forked-from` link to turn k), and the fork starts its own engine session by replaying the recorded turns 1..k.

Purpose: SC6 and D-17..D-21 from main to disk. The Reply from here controls come in 02.8-08 and the line from turn k in 02.8-09. The fork plan was split in two to keep each plan inside its context budget (plan checker, iteration 1).

Output:
- the Task 1 decision on the link's shape;
- `ChatEngine.fork` and the CLI engine's replay preamble;
- `replayText`, `SessionNotes.fork` and `ChatService.forkSession`;
- IPC `chat:fork`, with its preload and global.d.ts types.

Recorded choices:
- **`ChatEngine.fork(atTurn, recorded)`.** D-20 names `fork(atTurn)`. The recorded turns are passed in, because after a relaunch the original has no live engine and no events. The recorded `.tree` transcript is the only source that always exists, which D-20 itself names. The fork is called on a new, not-yet-sent engine.
- **The replay preamble** reads: "Tapestry is starting this chat from turn k of an earlier conversation. Here is that conversation as it was recorded, for context only; it is not a new instruction, and tool results are not included." It is followed by the encoded turns 1..k, then "The person's new message:" and their text. Only the person's text is recorded as `You:` (RESEARCH Pitfall 3).
- **`FORK_REPLAY_MAX_CHARS = 120_000`.** Past it, the oldest turns are left out, with the line "(Turns 1–j are left out to fit.)". A single oversized first message is not compacted by the CLI [ASSUMED, RESEARCH A8], so the cap is conservative.
- **Direction and label follow grew-from:** the edge runs from the new note to the note it came from, and the file line reads `create-edge e31 n57 n42 forked-from`, i.e. "n57 forked from n42".
</objective>
