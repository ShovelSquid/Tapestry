# Phase 02.8 Plan 05: Status, renderer half Summary

**Every session card and the enlarged view now show the state from 02.8-04's reducer: a 16px glyph, the state word and a one-line status line in a shared `ChatSessionHeader`. The card adds the looks: Working blurs and desaturates the transcript under a sharp header, Needs you gets a "!" badge and an author-colour border until a reply, Done gets the author-colour border until you look, and Failed shows its word and phrase in destructive text. The card also handles acknowledgement and LiveAnnouncer announcements. All of it is renderer state.**

## Accomplishments

- **The store** (`state/chat-sessions.ts`).
  - Each snapshot has `status: SessionStatus`. `applyChatPayload(snapshot, payload, now = Date.now())` runs `reduceStatus` on every event and still never mutates the old snapshot.
  - `openedStatus` builds the status when `chat.open` answers. It starts from `initialStatus({ busy, lastStatus, turns: 1 })`, so a relaunch is Idle with the last status and never Working (D-12). If the session is busy, it folds the running turn's live events on top.
  - `EMPTY_CHAT_SESSION.status` is Idle with no text.
  - Also added: `acknowledgeChatSnapshot`, `acknowledgeSession` and `statusAnnouncement`, plus `setSessionAgentOrder`, `sessionAuthorToken` and `useSessionAuthorToken`. `sendChatSession` acknowledges before it sends.
- **ChatSessionHeader** (new). Row 1 is the state glyph, the title slot and the caller's buttons. Row 2 is the status line.
  - **Glyphs:** a spark in `--tap-muted` for Idle and Working; "!" in a circle and a check in a circle for Needs you and Done, both in `--tap-session-author`; × in a circle in `--tap-destructive-text` for Failed.
  - **Status line:** Label size, muted, one line with an ellipsis, and the full text in `title`. The card shows `cardText` and the panel shows `text`. When Failed, the line is in `--tap-destructive-text`. It shows "New chat" when the text is empty and the note has no turns.
- **The card.**
  - It renders the header with its editable title input.
  - It sets `--tap-session-author: var(<token>)` inline and adds the state class. It draws `NeedsYouBadge` while `status.needs` is set.
  - It acknowledges after the pointer rests for 1000 ms, on a press or focus inside, and on Enlarge.
  - It announces changes into Done, Needs you or Failed at level 1 or above, through `useAnnounce()`.
  - The glyph, badge and border still show when the card is closed.
- **The panel.** It renders the same header (with `rowClassName="tapestry-chat-title-row"` and the author colour on `.tapestry-chat-header`). Opening it acknowledges Done and Failed. It has no blur, no badge and no announcements.
- **App.tsx** calls `setSessionAgentOrder(orderAgentsByConnection(agents))` whenever `agents` changes.
- **App.css.**
  - Glyph and status-line rules.
  - Working: `saturate(0.2) blur(2px)` on the transcript and `saturate(0.2)` on the composer, with a 200 ms ease filter transition. The blur goes to 0 on `:hover` and `:focus-within`.
  - Needs you and Done: a 2px `--tap-session-author` border, except on a selected card or connect target.
  - `.tapestry-needs-badge`: a 24px disc at (-12px, -12px) with a white "!" and the control-bubble shadow.
  - A reduced-motion rule turns the filter transition off.

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter. In short:
- **"New chat" is decided in the header**, not the store. The previous wave's handoff asked for the turn count to reach `initialStatus`. The store cannot know it: the note belongs to the tree, and a child composer's effect asks for the session before its card does. So the store opens with `turns: 1` (no last status gives an empty text), and the header shows "New chat" when that text is empty and the note's `committedTurns` is 0. The result is what the handoff wanted: an empty session says "New chat", and a relaunched one shows its last status.
- **A press inside the card acknowledges**, following the UI-SPEC's "click or focus anything in it".
- **The rest timer** is keyed on pointer-inside and state together, so a pointer that is already resting counts when Done arrives.
