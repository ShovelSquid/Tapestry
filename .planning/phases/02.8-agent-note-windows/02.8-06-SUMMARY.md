# Phase 02.8 Plan 06: Attention (levels, threshold, stack raise, edge arrows) Summary

**A session that finishes, fails, needs you or was just created while its card is off-screen now gets a 32px edge arrow in its author colour on the side facing it. Clicking the arrow glides the view there through Canvas's CameraRig. A state change at level 1 or more raises the card in its frame (renderer-only order). Done and level-3 changes jiggle and flash once when their level is at or over a new Chat alerts threshold (1-3, default 2) in the Agents panel. Failed never moves, and nothing an agent does moves the camera.**

## Accomplishments

- **Edge arrows (Task 1, tracer).**
  - `layout/edge-arrows.ts` is pure.
    - A card is off-screen when the screen bounding box of its four corners, taken through `worldToScreen`, misses the visible area. That area is the viewport minus the 436px panel column when a panel is open.
    - An arrow sits where the ray from the visible area's centre toward the card's centre crosses the inset track: 32px from the sides and bottom and 80px from the top, measured to the arrow's centre.
    - Arrows on one edge are spread 40px apart, in order and inside the edge. Crowded groups are centred on where their arrows wanted to be.
    - After spreading, each chevron is turned to point from its final spot to its card.
  - `EdgeArrowLayer` is the last child of the canvas viewport, outside the transform: after the canvas in DOM order and before the ChatPanel, with z-index 90, under the forest bar, panel and dialogs.
    - Each arrow is a `<button>` in the session's author colour. It shows a chevron, "!" on a filled disc for Needs you, or × for Failed.
    - `title` and `aria-label` read "Go to <title> — Done / Needs you / Failed / New chat".
    - Arrows fade in over 150ms. A leaving arrow fades out in place for 150ms and cannot be clicked.
  - Canvas collects every top-level session card of every open tree in world coordinates: frame origin plus drawn spot, at the measured size (else 360 × 440). It renders the layer, and `panToSession` centres the card in the visible area at the same zoom and roll.
- **Levels and threshold (Task 2).**
  - `changeEffects(prev, next, threshold)` decides motion and raise for each change.
  - The store keeps `lastEffects` on each snapshot, from `applyChatPayload`, under `currentAlertThreshold()`.
  - The Agents panel has a "Chat alerts" select below "Let agents connect", with the help line from the copy table.
  - The card plays `tapestry-session-card--arrive` for 1200ms: a `rotate` jiggle over 480ms and an author-colour flash ring over 1200ms. It is added only for a new `lastEffects` object whose `animate` is true.
  - The "!" badge scales in once, 0.6 to 1 over 240ms.
  - Done gets `--done-settled` 30 s after `status.arrivedAt`, and the border colour then fades over 2 s.
- **Stack raise (Task 3).**
  - `raiseInOrder` and `sortWithRaise` are added.
  - A per-tree raise order lives in the store and is raised from `recordChatPayload` when `raisesCard` holds.
  - TreeFrame's top-level card sort is now `sortWithRaise(…, depthOf, raiseOrder)`.
  - The card raises on a press anywhere in it and on focus.
  - TreeFrame gives focus back to whatever held it when a reorder moved its node.

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter. The two the plan asked to record:

- **Pan method.** The pan uses Canvas's CameraRig: `rig.easeTo(centerOn…)` with `FLY_TAU_MS`, then `kick()`, as `panToFrame` does, or `rig.direct` plus `showDrawn()` under reduced motion. It does not use `flyTo` from `threads/navigation.ts`, and it does not use `panToNote`, which ignores roll. This departs from the UI-SPEC Motion row, which named `flyTo`. The plan overrode that row on purpose (RESEARCH Pitfall 11).
- **Chat alerts options.** 1 "Move for every status", 2 "Move for done and needs you" (the default), 3 "Move for needs you only". The UI-SPEC row says "levels 0-3", but level 0 never animates, so the control offers only these three.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Correctness] `ChatContext.panelOpen` instead of `openSession !== null`**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The docked panel also opens for the workspace chooser and the no-workspace text (`ChatPanelState` kinds `choose` and `none`). `openSession` is null for both, so arrows would have sat under the panel.
- **Fix:** Added `panelOpen: boolean` to `ChatContextValue` (default false). App sets it to `chatPanel !== null`.
- **Files:** app/src/renderer/state/chat.ts, app/src/renderer/App.tsx (neither was in the plan's file list)
- **Commit:** 22bcb96

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Focus would have cleared a new chat's arrival at once**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** `createChatSession` asks the new card's composer to take focus, and the card acknowledges on focus. `isNew` would have been cleared before the person saw where the card landed, so the "New chat" arrow (A-08) could never show.
- **Fix:** `acknowledgeChatSnapshot` / `acknowledgeSession` take `{ keepNew }`, and the card's focus handler passes it. Pointer rest (now also counted for a new chat), a press, Enlarge and a send still clear it.
- **Files:** chat-sessions.ts, ChatSessionCard.tsx (the card was not in Task 1's file list)
- **Commit:** 22bcb96

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] A raise could have taken focus away**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** Raising by DOM order moves the raised card's node, and a moved node loses focus. Tabbing into a lower card, or a status raising the card you are typing in, would have dropped focus to the body.
- **Fix:** When TreeFrame's raise order changes, it records `document.activeElement` and restores it in a layout effect if the move took it away.
- **Files:** TreeFrame.tsx
- **Commit:** 8c66272

**4. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The global reduced-motion rule no longer ended App.css**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The ws/ui merge appended the "Motion flourishes" and selection-bar sections after it, so the acceptance check (`tail -n 30 … | grep prefers-reduced-motion`) failed and the plan's "verify that rule still ends the file" did not hold.
- **Fix:** Moved the block, with its comment, to the end of the file. All its declarations are `!important`, so the move changes no cascade result. The brace count is still balanced (340/340).
- **Commit:** 87f5410

### Other notes (signature choices)

- `arrowSpot(target, visible)` has no `panelOpen` parameter. With the panel open, `visible.right` is already `width − 436`, so the arrow centre at `visible.right − 16 − 16` equals the plan's `1000 − 452 − 16`. A third parameter would have been redundant.
- `spreadOnEdge(spots, spacing, segment)` also takes the edge's segment. The test needs its bounds, and the plan's two-argument form had no way to know them.
- Arrows are sorted by their position along the edge. On one edge this is the same order as their angles, and it has no wrap-around at ±180° on the left edge.
- `useSessionAttentions` returns attention plus the session's author token. The author colour comes from the store's agent name, which Canvas does not have, so the card props carry no `agent`.
- **Extra exports:**
  - edge-arrows.ts: `screenBounds`, `arrowTrack` and `ARROW_MIN_LEVEL`.
  - session-status.ts: `ChangeEffects`, `Attention` and `AttentionKind`.
  - chat-sessions.ts: `SessionEffects`, `SessionAttention`, `sessionAttention`, `sessionAttentions`, `DEFAULT_ALERT_THRESHOLD`, `currentAlertThreshold`, `raiseOrderFor` and `raisesCard`.
- **`changeEffects` for a send.** Entering Working counts as a level-1 change, so a send raises the card but never moves it. A repeat set_status with `needs: true` while already in Needs you is judged by its own level, not 3.
- **UI-SPEC compared with the current code.** The session card still uses the plain `.tapestry-note-card` surface, so the author borders, flash ring and arrow colours work as specified. No token was added. The ws/ui `look/motion` settings (`--tap-motion-swell`) do not affect the chat motion. Only the Chat alerts threshold and reduced motion govern it.

## Judgement calls for human review (unattended run)

1. **Done's settled look keeps a 2px border** in `--tap-border` until acknowledged, rather than going back to 1px. This avoids a second 1px content shift. The UI-SPEC says "back to 1px".
2. **Focus does not clear "New chat"**, but a pointer resting on the new card for a second does.
3. **A send raises the card** (it enters Working at level 1). Stop this if raising on your own send feels wrong.
4. **Needs you jiggles and flashes**, as well as scaling in the badge, because the levels table gives level 3 "jiggle + flash: always". The Motion table lists only the badge's scale for Needs you.
5. **The edge-arrow tooltip is the native `title`**, not a `.passage-chooser`-surface tooltip.
6. **Arrows near a corner, on two different edges, can overlap.** Spreading works per edge only.
7. **A card too small to read at far zoom still counts as visible**, so it gets no arrow (the planner's accepted limit).
8. **Arrows sit at z-index 90**, under the selection bar (100), so a bottom-edge arrow can be covered by the selection bar while notes are selected.
