<objective>
Turn the tracer's thin scan and index into the real read model: a read-only walk that honours every skip rule and changes nothing on disk, stable layout math with computed (never written) placement, and an Obsidian-rules link resolver with a full link index and backlinks.

Decisions honored: D-03 (a folder is a graph and its notes are cards: every directory reached is a graph in `Scan.folders`, every `.md` a note), D-04 (card rects read from frontmatter `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`), D-05 (stable hand-rolled FNV-1a hash of the normalized vault-relative path, identical across runs and machines; folder world position read from `world-x`, `world-y`, `world-scale` in `Folder/Folder.md`), D-06 (cross-folder resolution of `[[Folder/Note#^id|description]]` and plain `[[Note]]`), D-08 (the shared empty-spot finder `place_free` is what Phase 4 uses to put source cards near the prompting note; frontmatter keys such as `found_from` are carried through untouched).

Purpose: Phase 2's explorer and Phase 3's canvas render straight from `VaultIndex`; Phase 3 links through `link_path`/`build_link`.
Output: full `scan.rs`, new `layout.rs`, full `index.rs`, the fixture vault and the scan/index tests.
</objective>
