## Phase Goal

> The world's content, relationships, changes, and history live in a durable `.tree` journal a person can read without Tapestry — and unknown plugin data stays readable and safe

**Mode:** mvp — final expansion slice: the three kinds of time (TREE-04), the frozen human-readable golden fixture and the format guide (TREE-01), with the cold-read human check harvested at end of phase.

<objective>
Make the readability promise concrete and testable: prove the three kinds of time are distinguishable and that corrections preserve history; generate a golden `example.tree` under a fixed clock and freeze it as a byte-exact fixture with literal grep assertions; write `FORMAT.md` so a person with no Tapestry can read any `.tree` file cold.

Purpose: TREE-01 and TREE-04 are user-facing guarantees, not implementation details — the fixture and guide are the artifacts a human actually judges, and the verifier's end-of-phase UAT reads them.
Output: `kernel_tests/readability_test.cpp`, `docs/tree/example.tree`, `docs/tree/FORMAT.md`, extended `kernel_tests/kernel_test.cpp`.
</objective>

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-1-09 | Tampering | `.tree` digest chain (documented in FORMAT.md) | medium | accept | v1 digests are integrity-only: they detect accidental damage and casual edits, not deliberate forgery; no HMAC/signature is in scope; FORMAT.md states this explicitly so no reader assumes authenticity (Task 3) |
| T-1-08 | Information disclosure | plaintext journal and fixture | low | accept | Readability without the app is the product requirement; the fixture contains only invented example data; encryption is out of v1 (Task 3 "Not in v1") |
| T-1-17 | Repudiation | fixture regeneration path (`TAPESTRY_REGEN_FIXTURE`) | low | mitigate | Regeneration only when the env var is explicitly set; the default test run compares and fails on drift; fixture changes are visible in git diffs (Task 2) |
