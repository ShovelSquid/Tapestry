## Issues Encountered

- **The naive oracle in `letters.test.ts` had its own bug, twice**, before the property test passed: (1) an off-by-one between ProseMirror's 1-based document positions and the oracle's 0-based array indices, caught by a genuinely wrong insert-ordering result; (2) the oracle's "touched letters" scan only checked `[from, to)`, missing a mid-grapheme *insertion* (a zero-width range) entirely, caught by a letter-count mismatch. - **The 10,000-iteration property test was initially far too slow** (minutes, not seconds) because `assertMatches` called `expect().toBe()` inside an O(total-letters-so-far) loop, itself inside the 10k-iteration loop -- vitest's matcher overhead dominates at that call volume. - **The bench harness's debug side camera needed three iterations to frame content correctly**: the first attempt centered on the wrong world axis (content advances along Z, not X); the second forgot to reset the floating origin left over from an earlier `buildLoad()` call, placing everything off-frustum; the third revealed that showing the whole ~26s stretch at once makes glyphs fail the live-view's own legibility gate (by design, at that zoom, `size/upp` falls below the gate's threshold) -- resolved by adding a focused per-marker close-up camera mode alongside the whole-stretch overview, rather than fighting the legibility gate.
