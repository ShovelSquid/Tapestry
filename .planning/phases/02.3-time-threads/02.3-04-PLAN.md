<objective>
**As a** person writing into a thread, **I want to** watch my words stream away from me down a line that marks every second I spent writing, **so that** I can see the shape of the session I am living in as I live it.

This plan expands the tracer outward into the live z-axis view: the WebGL stage below the typer, the procedural ribbon, sharp letters, the pause slowdown, and the distance fade.

Purpose: this is the half of the feature that is not a file format — the thing Kaelen described as "a different way to write". Spikes 001, 002b, 003b and 004 already proved it can be drawn at 60fps; this plan ports their validated code into the app and carries their measured costs as constraints.

Output: the stage renderer, ribbon, glyph layer with its off-main-thread MSDF cache, the live camera derived from the thread's stored frame, and the scripted benchmark harness.
</objective>
