Blob Hands

Turn your hand to sweep the cursor: right hand facing the camera is the right edge, turned away is the left. Tilt for up/down; moving your hand pans on top. Calibrate (K) for precise aim. Make a fist to grab. Drag slowly to move a blob, yank fast to tear a piece off, push pieces together to merge them. Mouse and multi-touch work too.

Aim with
Point your nose at things. Make a fist (either hand) or hold Space to grab. Press C while looking straight ahead to recenter.
Hand tuning
Turning (hand angle)
Moving (hand position)

Start = where the cursor sits with your right hand facing the camera (the left hand mirrors it). Both add up: turning sets where the cursor points, moving your hand nudges it from there. Turning sensitivity is a multiple of your calibrated range (1 = the turn you calibrated spans the screen); moving is screens per camera width. 0 turns either off.
